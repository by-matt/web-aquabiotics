Technology

Dual Cascade Biorefinery

A modular DeepTech architecture that fractionates industrial liquid waste directly at the source.

01

Pre-treatment / UF

Centrifugation and Ultrafiltration to remove solids, protect membranes, and extract an initial massive protein concentrate.

02

Nanofiltration

Advanced physical fractionation without solvents. Isolates low molecular weight molecules like taurine, retaining and concentrating pure bioactives.

03

Fermentation

Biotransformation of residual nutrients to produce a complex of Parabiotics and Postbiotics of high immunological value.

04

Spray Drying

Final stabilization of liquid extracts into a micro-encapsulated powder, ensuring purity and commercial shelf life.

Origins

The Los Lagos Paradox

Aquabiotics Sur was born in the heart of the Los Lagos Region in southern Chile, a territory known for its pristine fjords and thriving aquaculture industry. A paradox was recognized: while the mussel industry is a major economic driver, it also generates significant volumes of liquid waste that are currently discarded.

This was seen not as waste, but as an untapped reservoir of valuable marine biomolecules. Our mission is to lead the Blue Circular Economy through marine biotechnology.

Mission

To lead the Blue Circular Economy by sanitizing the coasts, replacing synthetics with sustainable purity, and positioning Patagonia as a global hub for biotech innovation.

Vision

To be the global leader in upcycled marine metabolites, setting the standard for circular economy and biotech excellence from the heart of Patagonia.

Location

Strategic operations in Los Lagos Region, Chile—the hub of the Chilean mussel industry.
